You are reverse-engineering an unwritten annotation convention of the Cloudscape dataset.

Annotators watched AWS "This is My Architecture" videos and encoded each architecture as a
directed graph of services (edges "A -> B"). The written guidelines say the graph should
capture the full call graph of requests and responses, but annotators applied this
unevenly: in some graphs most connected pairs appear in BOTH directions (A -> B and B -> A:
responses, reads, results flowing back); in others every edge goes in ONE direction only.

Below are <N_EJEMPLOS> videos in two groups:
  GROUP ONE_WAY  — the annotation has no pair in both directions.
  GROUP TWO_WAY  — most connected pairs were annotated in both directions.
For each video you get its title, the transcript of the narration, and the annotated edges.

Your task: find what in the VIDEO (the narration and the kind of system described) predicts
the group. Propose up to 5 hypotheses. Each hypothesis must be:
  - a yes/no question that a reader can answer from the title and transcript ALONE, without
    seeing the graph (the annotated edges will not be available when it is applied);
  - concrete enough that two readers would give the same answer;
  - stated with the group that a "yes" predicts (two_way = true means "yes" predicts TWO_WAY).

Do not use general knowledge about how AWS services usually interact; the question must be
decidable from what is said in the video. Prefer hypotheses that hold for most examples in
both groups, and say in `razon` which examples contradict each one. If you find no signal,
say so in `analisis` and propose your best candidates anyway.
